What Omokboard Is

Omokboard is a free site that gathers 12 long-loved board games, like Omok, Chess, and Yut Nori, so you can play them right in your browser. There is no app to install and no account to create, and it works the same on a PC or a phone.

On your own, you can play against the AI or work through puzzles like Sudoku, Minesweeper, and 2048. With a friend next to you, pick 2-player mode and take turns on one device. With a friend somewhere else, use online play and connect with a 6-character room code. Online play works in Omok, Connect 4, Reversi, Dots and Boxes, Chess, Tic-Tac-Toe, Alkkagi, Ttakji, Yut Nori, and Number Baseball.

All 14 games have a guide covering the rules and beginner strategy. If a game is new to you, give it a quick read before your first round. Knowing where people usually get stuck saves you a lot of fumbling.
